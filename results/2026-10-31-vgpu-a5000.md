# vGPU on one RTX A5000 with PAIS 3.0 Model Endpoints, measured 2026-10-31

Host: ESX 9.1.1 build 25782009, NVIDIA vGPU Manager 580.178.05 (NVIDIA AI Enterprise 7.8 host package), card in display-off ("compute") mode, SR-IOV on in the BIOS, graphics type Shared Direct, vGPU mode Mixed Size, ECC on. Guest: VKS node from VM class `a5000-vgpu-12c` (8 vCPU, 32 GiB reserved, profile `nvidia_rtxa5000-12c`), GPU Operator 25.10.1, guest driver 580.105.08 (`nvidia-vgpu-driver-ubuntu24.04` from nvcr.io/nvaie), licence NVIDIA Virtual Compute Server from the Cloud License Service (acquired in 5 s, 24 h lease). Model: Gemma 4 12B QAT Q4 GGUF from a private Harbor, llama.cpp `server-cuda-b11913`, 16k context, flash attention, reasoning budget 0. Load from a machine next to the lab with `gw-bench.py` through the PAIS gateway, 400 completion tokens per request, prompt 210 chars.

## One 12C slice alone on the card

| Load | vGPU 12C, 2026-10-31 | Passthrough, 2026-10-27 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 request, streaming | 68.9 tok/s, TTFT 0.20 s | 70.9 tok/s |
| 4 parallel, aggregate | 228.1 tok/s (57.0 per request) | 242.7 tok/s |
| 8 parallel, aggregate | 387.7 tok/s (48.5 per request) | 350.8 tok/s |

Slice memory: 8,733 MiB of 12,288 MiB in use with the model loaded (host side shows 11,201 MiB allocated to the slice; the ECC overhead is taken from the 12 GB). A single tenant on a 12C slice gets the whole card: the time-sliced scheduler (best effort) gives an idle neighbour's time to the active one.

Host view during the 4 and 8 parallel runs (`nvidia-smi` on the host, 5 s samples): GPU utilisation 92 to 96 %, power 222 to 229 W of the 230 W cap, 68 to 74 °C; idle between runs 0 %, 89 W, 57 °C.

## Two 12C slices, two endpoints (A and B)

Second endpoint: VM with the second slice created, node joined, driver, licence and model loaded, answering through the gateway after 9 minutes (nothing cached on the new node). Both VMs hold 12C slices on the one card (Mixed Size mode, two 12 GB framebuffers).

| Load | B alone | A and B at the same time | Sum of both | One slice alone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 request each, streaming | 59.2 tok/s, TTFT 0.19 s | A 29.0, B 30.4 tok/s, TTFT 2.1 to 2.9 s | 59.4 | 68.9 |
| 4 parallel each, aggregate | 228.6 tok/s | A 109.1, B 104.1 tok/s | 213.2 | 228.1 |
| 8 parallel each, aggregate | (not run) | A 88.7, B 88.5 tok/s, 36 s per batch | 177.2 | 387.7 |

Reading: with two active tenants the best-effort time-slice scheduler splits the card evenly (A and B within 5 % of each other in every row). Light load (one stream per tenant) costs about 14 % against a single tenant, moderate load (4 + 4) about 7 %, but at 8 + 8 the aggregate falls to less than half of what one slice alone delivers at 8 parallel: the two llama.cpp instances each run 8 slots and the GPU switches between the two VMs every time slice, so neither batch keeps the SMs busy. Time to first token under contention rises from 0.2 s to 2 to 3 s. Compare the DRA run of 2026-10-30 (two containers in one VM, time-slicing): 96 + 96 = 192 tok/s under load.

Repeat of the 8 + 8 run: A 88.7, B 88.7 tok/s aggregate, 36.1 s per batch, single streams 28.5 and 28.6 tok/s. Identical to the first run to the decimal, so the split is deterministic, not noise.

Host view (`nvidia-smi vgpu -u`): B alone 88 to 96 % SM; with both tenants active the two vGPUs alternate at roughly 59 % and 39 % per sample, i.e. the card is time-sliced between the VMs (best-effort scheduler, the default). Both slices allocated: 22,401 MiB of 23,028 MiB on the host.

## Mixed sizes, 12C + 8C + 4C on one card

Endpoint B was deleted; a chat endpoint on an 8C slice (Gemma 4 12B QAT, text-only GGUF without the vision projector, ctx 4096, class `a5000-vgpu-8c`) and an embeddings endpoint on a 4C slice (BAAI bge-m3 on the Infinity engine, class `a5000-vgpu-4c`, no engine override) were created. All three slices were allocated within a minute (host: 12C + 8C + 4C, Mixed Size mode). Per-slice framebuffer on the host during the run: 12C 9,836 MiB, 8C 7,611 MiB, 4C 2,496 MiB.

- **8C with Gemma 4 12B does not work:** the slice fills to 7,611 of 8,192 MiB and the gateway reports the route `UNAVAILABLE` (502 `INVALID_MODEL_CONFIGURATION`). The 7.2 GB GGUF plus CUDA context leaves no room for the KV cache and compute buffers even at 4k context. The engine pod sat in `CrashLoopBackOff` with 9 restarts in 28 minutes while the driver, toolkit and validator pods on the same node were all Running. Sizing lesson: a 12B Q4 model needs the 12C slice; 8C fits models up to about 5 GB of weights.
- **4C with bge-m3 works:** embeddings alone (4 workers, 16 texts per request, 30 s): 923 requests, 14,768 texts in 30.1 s = **491 texts/s**, median latency 0.11 s, p90 0.19 s, vector dim 1024.

| Load | alone | 12C chat + 4C embeddings at the same time |
|---|---|---|
| 12C: 1 request streaming | 68.9 tok/s | 69.0 tok/s (TTFT 2.0 s, the cold first request) |
| 12C: 4 parallel aggregate | 228.1 tok/s | 220.0 tok/s |
| 4C: embeddings 4 workers | 491 texts/s, median 0.11 s | 506 texts/s, median 0.11 s, p90 0.19 s |

Reading: a small embedding tenant next to the chat tenant costs the chat endpoint about 3 % and the embedding endpoint nothing measurable. The time-slice scheduler only hurts when two tenants both saturate the SMs (the 8 + 8 case above); an embedding workload with 0.1 s bursts fits into the gaps. Caveat: an unrelated appliance deploy was running on the same host and datastore during this run.
